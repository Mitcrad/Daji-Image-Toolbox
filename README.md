# Daji Image Toolbox
Automatically monitor a folder of your choice. Whenever a new image is added, you can choose from three automated actions:

1. **Automatically apply your preset watermark** while keeping the original image.
2. **Automatically crop the image to your specified aspect ratio** while preserving the original file.
3. **Automatically batch rename images** using your preferred naming rules.

Everything runs **locally on your computer**, and no files or information are uploaded or transmitted over the internet.

It is ideal for workflows that require repeatedly processing large numbers of newly added images—such as applying watermarks, batch renaming, or automatically cropping images to a consistent aspect ratio.

Once configured, the entire process runs automatically, saving you from repetitive manual work and freeing up your hands.
